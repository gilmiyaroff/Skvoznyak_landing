Can Kazakhstan-based businesses advertise?

On October 21, 2026 OpenAI announced a 26-country expansion. Kazakhstan is mentioned in external breakdowns of the new markets, while the official Ads Manager country list may take time to update. We verify eligibility, billing and target locations for your specific business before launch.

Do ads influence ChatGPT responses?

No. Ads are separate and clearly labeled, and advertisers do not gain access to private conversations.

What's the minimum budget?

Minimums depend on market and billing currency. For USD the reference daily minimum is $25; a seven-day pilot would be $175 in media plus KZT 100,000 for one month of SKVOZNYAK campaign management. We confirm terms in Ads Manager before launch.

Are you an official OpenAI partner?

No. SKVOZNYAK is an independent marketing service provider and does not represent or claim endorsement by OpenAI.
