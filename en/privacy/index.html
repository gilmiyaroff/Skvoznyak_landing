Privacy policy

Pre-launch draft. Before publicly collecting personal information, the site operator must identify the business and supply contact details for privacy requests.

1. Data collected

When requesting a demo, visitors provide their name, phone / WhatsApp number, optional company name and request category. Hosting providers may also process technical connection information.

2. Purpose

Contact information is used solely to respond to the requested demo, consultation or onboarding inquiry. Submission details are not published.

3. Processing and disclosure

Once configured, the secure Cloudflare Pages handler forwards submitted information to a designated Telegram chat. This may involve international data processing by the provider.

4. Retention and deletion

This website does not store applications in a dedicated database. The operator must define message retention with the receiving service prior to launch. Users may contact the operator about deletion and other statutory rights.

5. Data controller details

Required before publication: business / controller name, privacy contact email, relevant address and retention period. This document is not yet a final legal privacy notice.
